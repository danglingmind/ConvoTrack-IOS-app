## 4. Positioning map (text)

Axes: **during-ride group awareness** (low → high) vs **established trust / rating volume** (low → high).

- High trust, low group awareness: REVER, Scenic, calimoto, Detecht, Harley-Davidson (route planning, solo nav, community feeds).
- High trust, partial group awareness: Cardo Ride (falls-behind alerts, but tied to Cardo/community, CarPlay complaints), Life360 / WhatsApp / Google Maps (location dots, no ride structure, no regroup, no nav-for-the-group).
- Low trust, high group awareness: Koast, Qafilaa, Group Ride io, Convoy (cars), Ridrs, ConvoTrack — all < 30 ratings.
- Empty quadrant: **high group awareness + trusted, motorcycle-specific**. Nobody holds it.

## 5. The gap ConvoTrack can own

**"The ride leader's tool for keeping a group together"**, not another motorcycle GPS. Evidence:
- The big motorcycle apps sell routes and scenery ("Scenic", "curvy", "Plan, Ride & Track", "community") and their group-riding reviews say groups give up mid-trip (REVER 11314726772) or that friends' routes diverge (calimoto 14617422232).
- Pre-ride setup is the shared pain (Sena 12679202582, 13032659754): a 6-char code/QR join with no accounts-to-friend is a direct answer.
- Only Cardo Ride and indie apps mention "falls behind" detection; nobody pairs it with *one-tap regroup reasons* (fuel/food/photo) and a *ride summary showing widest group gap*. Those are concrete, ownable features.
- Market-specific openings: UK "rideout" SERP has no established app (top 5 are 0–2-rating apps + calimoto); India's direct rival Qafilaa targets Ladakh/Spiti expeditions, leaving weekend club rides open, and ConvoTrack is the cheapest paid option in IN (₹1,200/yr vs ₹1,349–4,999).
- Risk: in GB ConvoTrack's £39.99/yr is above REVER (£34.99). Koast is the closest copy threat (convoy map + PTT + Koast+ at the same $39.99) and is car-and-moto.

## 6. Phrases competitors overuse (avoid as headline/subtitle differentiators)

- "Motorcycle GPS" / "Motorbike GPS" / "Navigation" — in REVER, Detecht, calimoto, Scenic, Sygic, MyRide, RideOuts, MotoTrk names. Hard to rank, says nothing about groups.
- "Scenic" / "curvy" / "Routes" / "Plan, Ride & Track" — Scenic, calimoto, Detecht, Ridrs.
- "Community" / "connect with riders" — Cardo Ride, Harley-Davidson, REVER (and reviewers say local communities are empty).
- "Live tracking" / "live map" / "ride tracker" — Group Ride io, Qafilaa, MotoTrk, Convoy Tracker, Cardo; becoming generic among indies. ConvoTrack's own subtitles ("ride tracker & map") sit in this crowded phrase.
- "Safety" / "crash detection" — Detecht, Qafilaa, Ajjas own it with crash detection, which ConvoTrack does not have.
- "Convoy … with friends" / "crew" — Convoy (cars), Koast.

Underused and tied to ConvoTrack's real features: "regroup", "keep the group together", "no one left behind", "ride leader", "rideout" (GB), "join with a code".
